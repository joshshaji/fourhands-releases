Fourhands

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Fourhands is a Mac app, made by Saaspero LLC, that gives AI coding agents their own copies of your apps on a hidden screen. It's built to keep your data on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

Everything Fourhands works with stays in Fourhands' folder (~/Library/Application Support/Fourhands), readable only by your user account:

  • your twins' browser profiles and copied app sessions, including what you sign in to there;
  • recipes your agents save (the steps, and what was typed unless it was turned into a blank);
  • the night-shift queue, each task's report and log;
  • the activity feed, which records what happened (an agent started a twin, opened a page, asked for you), never what was typed or what a page said;
  • Fourhands' settings and log.

We never receive any of it. Fourhands has no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting that sends data anywhere.

What Fourhands sends, and to whom

  • Subscription checks (RevenueCat). Fourhands asks RevenueCat, our payments provider, whether your account has Fourhands Pro. It sends only your Fourhands account code, a random identifier made on your Mac (like fourhands_…). No name, no email, nothing about your apps, agents or files.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat and Stripe). Checkout opens in your browser on RevenueCat's site. RevenueCat and its payment processor, Stripe, collect what checkout needs (such as your email, payment details and, for tax, your country or address) under their own privacy policies. We receive the purchase record (your account code, plan, dates and the email you gave at checkout) so we can support you.
  • Downloads and updates (GitHub). Fourhands' downloads and update files are hosted on GitHub. Every few hours Fourhands fetches a small file from GitHub to see whether there's a newer version, and downloads it if there is. Like any web request, this shows GitHub your IP address.

What your agents do

Fourhands acts on your agents' instructions. When an agent opens a website in a browser twin, that site receives ordinary browser traffic, as if you'd visited it. The agents you connect are separate products; what they send to their own services is governed by their terms and privacy policies, not this one.

Your choices

  • Remove a twin (Fourhands › Twins) to delete its profile and sign-ins.
  • Delete recipes from Fourhands › Recipes; clear the activity feed from Fourhands › Activity.
  • To remove everything: quit Fourhands, delete it from Applications, and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Fourhands.
  • To ask about, correct or delete your purchase records, contact us below.
