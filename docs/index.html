The wall every agent hits: it needs the app you're using.

Computer-use agents drive your real windows, mouse and keyboard. So the moment your agent needs Chrome or Slack, it has to ask you to stop using them, and you sit and wait.

Fourhands ends the contention. Each agent gets its own signed-in copy of the app on a screen only it sees. Nothing it does touches your windows, and when only you can do something, the twin comes to you for a moment and goes back.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your agent

    One click adds Fourhands to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Claude for Mac. Any other MCP client works too.

  2. 2

    It starts a twin when it needs one

    Its own Chrome profile, its own copy of Slack, or its own window of TextEdit, Finder or Mail. You approve each app the first time.

    You only step in for what's yours

    A sign-in, a two-factor code, pressing Send or Pay. A one-line note brings the twin to your screen; press Done and it goes back.

Your apps, twice

Browser twins have their own profile, so you sign in once where your agent needs. App twins like Slack start from a copy of your session. Your own apps are never changed.

Any Mac app

Agents ask for apps by name. They get their own window of your TextEdit, Finder, Mail or Xcode on the hidden screen, with menus they can use, or take turns with you in one-window apps.

Screen codec

After an agent's first look at a page, Fourhands sends only what changed: new controls, new text, and pictures of just the changed part of the screen. Every control keeps its number.

Recipes

An agent does a task once and saves it. Next time Fourhands replays every step in seconds without the model, checks each one first, and hands back the moment a page has changed.

Night shift

Queue work for tonight. While you sleep, an agent does it in its twins with your Mac to itself. Sign-ins, Send, Pay and Delete wait for you, and the morning brings a review.

You stay in charge

Fourhands asks you before an agent presses Send, Pay or Delete, never types into password or card fields, and can't see or touch your own windows, mouse or keyboard.

Questions

Which agents does Fourhands work with?

Anything that speaks MCP. Fourhands connects Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Claude for Mac in one click, and shows the settings for any other client.

Does my agent see my screen?

No. It sees only its own twins, on a screen that exists while Fourhands runs and that no monitor shows. Your windows, mouse and keyboard are never part of it.

What does Fourhands need permission for?

Accessibility, to move app twins and to work native-app windows like TextEdit and Finder. Screen Recording is optional, for pictures of those windows. Browser twins need neither.

What happens when the trial ends?

Agents can't use twins until you subscribe. Everything you set up stays: twins, sign-ins, recipes and your night-shift queue.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes. Copy the account code from Fourhands' Account page and paste it into Fourhands on your other Mac.
